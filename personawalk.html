personawalk

Pedestrian Route Choice Simulation Using Persona-informed AI Agents.

Route-choice modelling underpins research on walkability, accessibility, and outdoor thermal comfort — yet most approaches rely on representative agents following utility-maximising rules, averaging away the differences between individual walkers. This work gives each agent a persona and lets it choose its own way through the city.
